# Consolidated Claim Ledger

## Supported as architecture / benchmark construction
- EAF has been specified as an administration and runtime-control plane.
- Deterministic benchmark generators exist for authority/evidence/context and
  administrative-control scenarios.
- The combined controlled corpus contains 4,400 episodes across five domains.
- The benchmark contains held-out, adversarial and falsification subsets.
- Canonical benchmark truth is not generated by an evaluated LLM.

## Supported as observed real runtime evidence
- One preserved EIA paired evaluation records pinned workload parity.
- Both arms record successful execution for that evaluation.
- Outcome quality is recorded as 70/100 vs 70/100.
- Context estimates are recorded at ~9,895 vs ~408 tokens at the application/runtime boundary.
- Synchronous EIA microkernel overhead is recorded as 8,450.429 ms.
- The source claim gate remains closed.

## Not yet supported
- EAF improves quality.
- EAF improves security or compliance versus a baseline.
- EAF reduces provider-metered model input.
- EAF reduces end-to-end latency.
- EAF reduces cost.
- EAF produces enterprise ROI.
- EAF is production-proven cross-vendor.
