**Status:** FREEZE CANDIDATE

## Research proposition

Enterprise agents require an explicit administration and runtime-control plane
between probabilistic intelligence and authoritative enterprise systems.

The Administration Plane controls **who/what is registered, enabled, scoped,
admitted, approved, delegated, revoked, or disabled**.

The Runtime Control Plane enforces those administrative decisions during a
specific mission and records the resulting authority/decision lineage.

The framework does not replace enterprise-system authorization, system-of-record
truth, human accountability, or model intelligence.

## Truth boundary

Benchmark truth is produced deterministically from canonical administrative state.
The evaluated model cannot define expected labels.

## No empirical superiority claim

This R1.1 build constructs and validates the benchmark only.
