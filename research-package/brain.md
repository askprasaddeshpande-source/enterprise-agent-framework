# EAF Research Brain

The core research problem is not whether an LLM can call enterprise tools.
It is whether an enterprise can **administer non-human actors** with the same
discipline applied to identities, APIs, systems, policies and production change.

The strongest EAF proposition is therefore:

> Enterprise AI requires a dedicated administration and runtime-control plane
> between probabilistic intelligence and authoritative enterprise systems.

The most important falsifiable boundary remains:
**capability != authority**.

The evidence program has three layers:

1. **EAFBench** — deterministic controlled correctness.
2. **EAF-OPS** — observational runtime feasibility/characterization.
3. **Future governed paired cohorts** — causal enterprise-value evaluation.

Do not collapse these evidence classes.
