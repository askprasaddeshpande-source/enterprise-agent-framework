## Scope

This repository/release is the public reproducibility artifact for the EAF research program. It preserves the frozen scientific source, experiment protocols, benchmark material, result ledgers, deterministic zero-call forensics, validation tooling, and publication evidence needed to audit the reported results.

The research snapshot is preserved byte-for-byte under esearch-package/. Publication packaging does not rerun experiments, make model calls, perform enterprise writes, or mutate frozen evidence.

## Scientific boundary

The evidence supports bounded authority-aware context-compilation utility and cross-model replication of the MAWS utility-preservation direction. It **does not establish general enterprise safety or cross-model zero-critical-action safety**. The frozen R3 conclusion remains INTEGRATED_ASSURANCE_SAFETY=NOT_ESTABLISHED.
