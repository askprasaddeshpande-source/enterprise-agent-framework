## Core empirical boundary

MAWS reduced model-active input while preserving available required evidence and controlling authority in the frozen benchmark, and its task-utility advantage over approximately length-matched generic pruning replicated directionally across the tested model families. Full EAF reduced unauthorized-action rate across R3 model blocks, but critical unauthorized actions remained in three of four model blocks; hard safety is therefore **not established**.
